## What Is SILENT HILL: Townfall?

SILENT HILL: Townfall is a psychological horror game developed by Screen Burn Interactive and co-published by KONAMI and Annapurna Interactive. It expands the Silent Hill universe with a first-person perspective, an isolated Scottish island, unsettling environments, and a mystery built around the town of St. Amelia.

The story follows Simon Ordell, who returns to the island with a personal reason to set things right. As he moves through fog-covered streets, abandoned buildings, and unfamiliar locations, fragments of the town's past begin to emerge.

A distinctive device called the CRTV plays an important role in exploration and discovery. This pocket television receives unstable signals that can reveal clues, point toward nearby threats, and help Simon understand the events unfolding around him.

The experience combines environmental investigation, narrative puzzles, stealth, and tense encounters. Players must decide when to investigate, when to avoid danger, and when a direct confrontation may be necessary.

## Core Gameplay Features

### First-Person Exploration

The game presents its environments directly through Simon's perspective. This viewpoint places greater emphasis on close observation, environmental clues, sound cues, and the uncertainty of moving through unfamiliar locations.

### Psychological Horror

The atmosphere draws on isolation, distorted environments, unsettling imagery, and uncertainty. Rather than relying solely on encounters with hostile creatures, the game builds tension through the setting and the mystery surrounding St. Amelia.

### Narrative-Driven Puzzles

Puzzles connect with the story, the environment, and the people associated with the island. Their solutions can reveal information about the situation and help players understand what lies behind the town's unsettling appearance.

### Exploration and Discovery

Players investigate streets, buildings, shorelines, and other locations throughout St. Amelia. Paying attention to objects, signals, and environmental details helps reveal clues and possible routes forward.

## Simon Ordell and St. Amelia

### Simon Ordell

Simon is the central character whose return to St. Amelia drives the story. His connection to the island gradually becomes clearer as he investigates its history and encounters evidence of its troubled past.

### The Isolated Scottish Island

St. Amelia is a remote island setting surrounded by cold waters and dense fog. Its quiet streets and seemingly abandoned locations create an atmosphere of uncertainty throughout the journey.

### Fragments of the Past

Clues found during exploration gradually reveal details about the island and its inhabitants. The narrative encourages players to connect information from different locations rather than treating every discovery as an isolated event.

### A Mystery With Consequences

The story is shaped by discoveries, character interactions, and player decisions. Different narrative outcomes give importance to the choices made during the journey.

## The CRTV and Signal Investigation

### A Pocket Television With a Purpose

The CRTV is a portable television that receives unstable visual signals. It expands on the familiar idea of using electronic interference to sense danger, giving players another way to interpret their surroundings.

### Detecting Nearby Threats

The device can help reveal threats that are difficult to identify through ordinary observation. Using it while moving through uncertain areas adds another layer of awareness to exploration.

### Signals and Story Clues

Some broadcasts contain information related to the island's mystery. Examining these signals helps connect environmental discoveries with the wider narrative.

### Tuning and Signal Reception

Players can adjust the CRTV to improve reception and investigate unusual transmissions. Certain interactions use physical device movements, making signal investigation part of the hands-on puzzle experience.
